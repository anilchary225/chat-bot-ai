Data deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026

You can delete your Zenoxi data at any time.

Delete a chat

  1. Open zenoxi.vercel.app and sign in.
  2. In the sidebar, hover over (or tap) the chat and press the trash icon, then confirm Delete chat.

This permanently deletes the chat's messages and attached files, including messages that were sent from WhatsApp or Instagram, and unlinks that chat from those apps.

Delete your whole account

Email vadlaanilchary225@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Zenoxi account". If you only used WhatsApp or Instagram, include your WhatsApp number or Instagram username. We delete your account, chats, files and messaging app links within 30 days and confirm by email.

Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp

Zenoxi does not use Facebook Login. Data received through WhatsApp or Instagram (your messages, phone number or Instagram ID) is deleted by the steps above. You can also remove Zenoxi's access to your Instagram messages in Instagram: Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools.
