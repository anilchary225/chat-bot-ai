Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Zenoxi is an AI chat assistant available on the website zenoxi.vercel.app, on WhatsApp and on Instagram. This policy explains what information Zenoxi collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you use "Continue with Google", we also receive your Google account ID and profile picture.
  • Conversations: the messages you send to Zenoxi and its replies, on the website, WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Messaging app identifiers: if you chat on WhatsApp, your phone number; on Instagram, your Instagram-scoped user ID. These are used only to reply to you and to keep your chat in sync.
  • Files you upload: the text extracted from documents you attach to a chat (for example PDF or Word files).
  • Resume tool: resumes and job descriptions you submit are processed to produce your result and are not stored after the request finishes.
  • Technical data: a sign-in token and display preferences (such as light/dark mode) are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in (we email you a one-time code or use Google sign-in).
  • To generate answers, keep your chat history and let you continue a conversation across the website, WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and do not use your conversations for advertising.

Services that process data for us

  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to: receive your messages (and relevant document text) to generate replies.
  • Web search providers (Tavily and public search engines): receive your question when you turn on web search.
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram): deliver messages between you and Zenoxi when you use those apps.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Brevo: sends sign-in code emails.
  • MongoDB Atlas, Render and Vercel: store data and host the service.

How long we keep it

Chats, messages and uploaded document text are kept until you delete them. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and sign-in sessions after 30 days. You can ask us to delete your whole account at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any chat in the app; this also deletes its messages and attached files.
  • Request a copy of your data, a correction or full account deletion by emailing us.
  • Stop using Zenoxi on WhatsApp or Instagram at any time by no longer messaging it.

See Data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes, and webhook requests from Meta are signature-checked. No online service is perfectly secure, so please don't share passwords, card numbers or other highly sensitive data in chats.

Children

Zenoxi is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
