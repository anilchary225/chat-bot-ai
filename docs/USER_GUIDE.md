# Zenoxi user guide

Zenoxi is an AI assistant you can use on the website, in Instagram DMs and on WhatsApp, with one shared conversation.

## Getting started

1. Open **https://zenoxi.vercel.app**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, or enter your email and tap **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from your inbox (check spam). Codes expire after 10 minutes.

No password is needed. To sign out, use the button next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Chatting

- Type in the message box and press **Enter** (**Shift + Enter** for a new line).
- Start a new chat with **New chat**, or **⌘K / Ctrl+K**.
- Your chats are in the sidebar, grouped by date. Use **Search chats** to find one, and the trash icon to delete it.
- **Copy** under an answer copies it; code blocks have their own Copy button.
- Switch light/dark mode with the sun/moon button at the top right.

> AI answers can be wrong. Check important facts.

## Live web search

Tap the **🌐 Web** button in the message box (it turns orange). Zenoxi searches the web before answering and lists its
**sources** under the answer; the **[1] [2]** marks in the text open the matching page. Use it for news, prices,
versions, and anything recent.

## Asking about documents

- Tap **📎** or drag files onto the chat. Supported: PDF, Word (.docx), TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON and code files,
  up to 10 MB each and 10 per chat.
- Once a file shows as ready, ask about it: *"Summarize this"*, *"What is the refund policy?"*
- Files stay attached to that chat, so later questions can use them too.
- Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) can't be read. Use a PDF with selectable text.

## Resume ATS Match

1. Open **Resume ATS Match** in the sidebar.
2. Upload your resume (PDF, Word or text) or paste it, then paste the full job description.
3. Tap **Analyze match** to see:
   - a **match score** (keywords and formatting)
   - **matched** and **missing** keywords
   - formatting checks, with tips
4. Tap **Generate tailored ATS resume**. Zenoxi rewrites your resume for the job **without inventing experience**:
   keywords you don't have are listed under **"Add only if true"**.
5. Review **Changes applied**, then **Download PDF** (fitted to one page) or **Word (.docx)**.

The score is an estimate; every company's ATS works a little differently. Your resume isn't stored.

## Website Agents: an AI assistant for your own website

1. Open **Website Agents** in the sidebar → **Create a new agent**. Enter your business name and website (optional).
2. **Setup:** answer Zenoxi's questions about your business, then tap **Build my agent** and review the profile
   (services, prices, hours, FAQs, policies, what never to promise). Save.
3. **Knowledge:** add more website pages, files (brochure, price list) or notes. The agent answers **only** from
   these.
4. **Customize:** name, colour, position, welcome message, suggested questions, and the **inquiry form**: a card or
   one question at a time, with your own fields.
5. **Test:** ask what customers would ask.
6. **Install:** add your website's domain, tap **Go live**, and paste the one-line code before `</body>` on your site
   (tips for WordPress, Wix and Shopify are on that screen). **Preview on a sample page** shows it before you install.
7. **Leads:** every inquiry appears here with an AI summary. Set a status (new → contacted → interested → won/lost),
   add notes, read the visitor's chat, and download **Excel** or **CSV**.
8. **Inbox:** questions your agent couldn't answer. Type the answer once and the agent knows it from then on.
9. **Emails:** choose what gets sent: an alert to you for each inquiry, a confirmation to the visitor (with your
   brochure if they ask for it), a follow-up after a few days, and a daily or weekly leads report. Visitors can always
   unsubscribe.
10. **Analytics:** messages, leads, conversion, top questions, and how many answers were free.

**Customize** also lets you pick an avatar (orb, initials, emoji or your logo), the widget language (English, Hindi,
Telugu, Tamil) and, optionally, add your own OpenRouter key. Each agent handles up to 100 visitor messages a day on
Zenoxi's AI, or 2,000 with your own key. To share a brochure, upload it in **Knowledge** and turn on **Email to
visitors**.

## One chat on the website, Instagram and WhatsApp

**Continue a website chat in Instagram or WhatsApp:**
1. Open the chat and tap the phone icon (**Continue on…**) at the top.
2. Tap **Open WhatsApp** (the code is pre-filled) or **Open Instagram DM**, and send `link CODE`.
3. Zenoxi replies "Linked". Keep chatting there; it remembers everything from the website.

**Continue an Instagram or WhatsApp chat on the website:**
1. Send `/web` to Zenoxi.
2. Open the link it sends, while signed in. The chat is added to your account and appears in your sidebar.

Messages from the apps appear in the open website chat within a few seconds, marked *via Instagram* or
*via WhatsApp*.

| Command (in Instagram or WhatsApp) | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help` | Show the commands |
| `/new` | Start a new chat |
| `/web` | Get a private link to this chat on the website |
| `/search your question` | Answer with live web results |
| `link CODE` | Continue a website chat |

**Keep `/web` links private:** the first signed-in person to open one gets the chat. After that, only that account
can open it.

> While Zenoxi is in its testing phase, Instagram replies work only for accounts added as testers, and WhatsApp is not
> open yet.
