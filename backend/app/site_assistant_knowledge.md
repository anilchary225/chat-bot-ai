## Zenoxi overview, services, and availability
Zenoxi is an AI workspace for AI chat, resume review and tailoring, and website AI agents. The landing page at https://zenoxi.vercel.app/ explains the tools. Compare features on the public pages or sign in to use the tools. Zenoxi is currently offered free of charge under its Terms of Service, subject to usage limits and availability. Do not promise unlimited usage, a paid plan, a free trial, support hours, or an interview. The landing assistant answers product and support questions; use the signed-in tools to chat with files, run a resume analysis, or build an agent.

## Getting started, AI chat, web search, and documents
AI Chat: https://zenoxi.vercel.app/ai-chat. Open the workspace at https://zenoxi.vercel.app/?app=true. Sign in with Google or a six-digit email code, and accept the Terms of Service before continuing. If the email is missing, check Spam or Promotions, or request a new code after the resend timer. If you do not have an account, choose Sign up. Zenoxi streams replies, keeps conversation history, and supports light and dark themes. Turn on Web search in the composer to get current answers with sources. Attach a PDF with selectable text, Word file, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, or supported code file, then ask a question about it. Chat supports up to 10 files per chat and 10 MB per file. Scanned PDFs may need a text version. Zenoxi can reply in the visitor's language and uses English letters for Telugu (Tenglish). Verify important AI answers.

## Resume analysis, ATS checks, tailoring, and PDF or Word export
Resume Tools: https://zenoxi.vercel.app/resume-tools. Open the tool at https://zenoxi.vercel.app/resume?app=true. Upload or paste your existing resume. Add a job description to analyze the overlap in keywords and review formatting checks. The ATS match score is an estimate; it does not guarantee a perfect score, passing an employer's system, or getting an interview. Tailor the draft to a job description or request a general version without one. Use only your real experience and add suggested skills only if true. Review the draft before downloading an A4 PDF or Word document. If a draft exceeds one page, shorten descriptions. Use the clear data action to remove the upload and generated draft from the tool after finishing.

## Website AI agents, knowledge, widget installation, and inquiries
Website Agents: https://zenoxi.vercel.app/website-agent. Open Agent Studio at https://zenoxi.vercel.app/agents?app=true. Create an agent for your business, review its profile, and add website pages, FAQs, files, or notes in Knowledge. The agent answers from the sources you provide. Customize the greeting, appearance, language, questions, and optionally an inquiry form. Use Test before publishing. In Install, add your website's domain, go live, then paste the supplied widget script on your site. If a widget is missing, check that its domain is allowed and its status is Live. Owners can review leads, export leads, answer previously unanswered questions in Inbox, set up email alerts and follow-ups, and view analytics. Features such as inquiry emails require owner setup. Visitor messages are rate limited.
